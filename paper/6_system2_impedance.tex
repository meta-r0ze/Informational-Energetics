Consequently, the total geometric impedance is the exact linear combination of the six pillar costs:
\begin{equation}

In any network of series constraints, the natural measure of throughput is not the resistance itself but the \textbf{admittance} $Y = 1/Z$. The $E_8$ substrate is not a thermal bath, but a maximally constrained, zero-flux causal network. On such a network, the fractional flow of information across a node (the causal coupling) is governed by the network's geometric admittance:

\subsection{The Resonant Circumference (Capacity)}
\label{subsec:alpha_cap}
Capacity measures the entropic footprint required for a topological defect to maintain structural coherence across its fundamental cycle. It measures the geometric cost of maintaining the spatial boundary that distinguishes a localized defect from the vacuum bulk. Without this cost, the state would propagate openly at the bandwidth limit ($c=1$) and delocalize completely.

% Establish the metric and evaluation domain
At Quiescent Equilibrium, the substrate's footprint is evaluated on its native positive-definite (Euclidean) metric required by Unitarity (\Cref{subsec:positive_definite}), with causal propagation restricted to a $(1+1)D$ plane $(x,\tau)$.

% Derive the maximal reach from periodicity
The fundamental cycle is periodic: the defect's configuration must recur identically after $\Delta$ discrete updates or it does not persist. At the network speed limit ($c=1$), a signal can traverse at most one lattice spacing per tick. To remain causally synchronized, a signal must be able to travel from the defect's central anchor to its boundary and back within this cycle. A round trip to a radial distance $r$ requires $2r$ ticks, bounding the maximal coherent radius to $r \le \Delta/2$.

\textbf{The continuous geometric measure.} The lattice provides a discrete address space, but the substrate's footprint is evaluated on its native positive-definite Euclidean metric (\Cref{subsec:positive_definite}). By Homogeneity, macroscopic observables must be invariant under lattice orientation. The invariant envelope (the union of all valid closed $\Delta$-cycles, averaged over all orientations) is therefore an isotropic disk of diameter $D = 2r = \Delta$. Any discrete anisotropy cancels in the orientation average, leaving the continuous Euclidean measure.

Because the defect is defined strictly by its topological boundary (\Cref{sec:topological_boundary}), and its interior is indistinguishable from the vacuum, the entropic cost is the measure of this continuous perimeter, not the discrete tick count. The geometric impedance is therefore the circumference of the isotropic envelope:
\begin{equation}
    Z_{\mathrm{CAP}} = C = \pi D = \pi\Delta \approx \num{\AlphaInvCAPVal}
\end{equation}

The dominant contribution to the vacuum impedance ($\approx 99.9\%$) originates from this fundamental geometry (this corresponds in the standard effective field theory dual to the Wilson Loop).

\subsection{The Additive Baseline and the Finite Sum}
\label{sec:additive_baseline}

In Quiescent Equilibrium, the six pillars operate on orthogonal, independent domains (\Cref{sec:entropic_balance_sheet}). This motivates the functional form of the total structural index:

\paragraph{Dimensionless Additivity and Unit Weighting}
In a closed, self-specifying substrate at Quiescent Equilibrium, the total Net Entropic Impedance $Z_{\mathrm{geo}}$ is the exact, unweighted linear combination of the six pillar impedances:
\begin{equation}
    Z_{\text{geo}} = \sum_{i=1}^6 s_i Z_i , \quad s_i \in \{+1, -1\} ,
\end{equation}
where direct structural costs enter with a positive sign and coordination investments enter with a negative sign.

Dimensionless Additivity and Unit Weighting rests on three substrate-universal identities dictated by the vacuum's Closure Constraint:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item{Universal Currency.} 
    Standard physics requires dimensional conversion constants ($\hbar, c, G$) because it measures heterogeneous phenomena in macroscopic unit conventions. On a self-specifying discrete substrate, no such units exist; the unique primitive quantity is the elementary state transition. The geometric perimeter ($\pi\Delta$), topological boundary ($\chi$), and phase-space fractions (PRO, GOV, TOL, MAR) are all pure dimensionless expectation values of these state transitions. Because they share an identical operational currency, their linear combination is mathematically well-defined without dimensional conversion factors.
    
    \item{Unit Weighting.}
    In standard statistical mechanics, independent subsystems carry weights (e.g., temperature, chemical potential) supplied by an external reservoir. By the Closure Constraint, the vacuum admits no external environment to configure arbitrary scaling ratios. A non-unity coefficient would introduce an unconstrained free parameter, structurally requiring a deeper layer to define it and thus violating self-specification. The structural coupling between orthogonal pillars is therefore fixed to exactly unity.
    
    \item{Complete Sign Assignment.}
    The six pillars mathematically exhaust the minimal architecture required for persistence. Their signs are uniquely fixed by the Entropic Balance Sheet (\Cref{sec:entropic_balance_sheet}): direct structural overheads (CAP, MAP, TOL, MAR) increase total impedance ($+$), while organizational feedback mechanisms (PRO, GOV) reduce net dissipation ($-$).
\end{enumerate}

The geometric impedance is therefore given by expanding this sum into the exact individual costs derived in \crefrange{subsec:alpha_cap}{subsec:alpha_mar}:

\begin{split}

+ \,\underbrace{\chi}_{\text{MAP}}
